Why SIBOL

Good help shouldn't depend on good signal

Lots of kids in the Philippines study where the internet is slow, costly or gone. That's exactly where they need help the most.

Offline first

Lessons, practice and explanations live on the phone. No signal? Class continues.

Speaks their language

English, Tagalog and Taglish everywhere, with answers Gabo can read out loud.

Private by design

No sign-ups. A child's progress stays on their device, and questions go to a model in the room.

Kalma
Three learning modes

One tutor. Three ways to learn.

Every child learns differently. Each mode changes how the app looks and how the AI talks.

Kalma

Calm and focused

A quiet space with fewer distractions, for learners who do best when things stay simple and predictable.

The screen

  • Plain background, no music
  • One question at a time
  • Same layout every time

The AI

  • Two short sentences, max
  • One idea per reply
  • Calm, gentle tone

Pakinig

Listen and hear it

For learners who find reading hard. Everything gets bigger, clearer and spoken out loud.

The screen

  • Large font, high contrast
  • Read-aloud, word by word
  • Answer with your voice

The AI

  • Written for the ear
  • No symbols to decode
  • Slower, clearer voice
3/4 becomes “three fourths”  ·  × becomes “times”

Hakbang

One step at a time

Big problems become small steps, with everyday Filipino examples and a quick check after each one.

The screen

  • Bigger step-by-step guide
  • Pictures before numbers
  • Review from earlier grades

The AI

  • Just the next step
  • Simple words
  • Ends with a check question

Each mode's rules are enforced in code, so a small model can't wander off. SIBOL supports different learning needs. It does not diagnose.
